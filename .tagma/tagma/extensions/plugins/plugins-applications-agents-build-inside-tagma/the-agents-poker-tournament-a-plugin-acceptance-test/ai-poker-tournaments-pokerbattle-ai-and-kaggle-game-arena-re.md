- PokerBattle.ai, 27–31 October 2025: nine models (o3, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Grok, DeepSeek R1, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Magistral, Kimi K2, GLM 4.6, Llama 4) played no-limit hold'em $10/$20 with play money at four tables of nine, 3,799 hands. o3 won, Llama lost its whole bankroll. Covered by poker.org (Octopi Poker analysis, 2025-12-03) and TechRadar (2025-12-06).
- From the analysis: models bluff poorly, often continue betting on the flop and fold on later streets; card hallucinations occur.
- Kaggle Game Arena added heads-up poker in February 2026; academic “Poker Arena” (arXiv 2606.13815) and PokerBench.

Takeaway for the video: the “models play poker” format has been shown and covered by press. Not established: the social-media reach of that coverage.
