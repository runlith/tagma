Operator: a tab with an artifact cannot be zoomed; ⌘+/⌘− with focus in it zooms all of Tagma. The document in the tab zooms with its own buttons and shortcuts, remembered per page; a shortcut from the page itself (iframe) is handed by desktop to the app by binding declaration, not by the View menu.
