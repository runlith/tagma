Limit, default, budget, timeout, and design are justified by the range of cores, memory, disks, repo sizes, thread and project counts, and operating systems. A product decision about Tagma and a recommendation concerning it start from all its users — from novice to expert, in projects with and without code; the runlith catalog, tags, and habits are one installation, not the reference. A local measurement is reported as one sample; a machine-dependent figure is derived from the executing machine.
