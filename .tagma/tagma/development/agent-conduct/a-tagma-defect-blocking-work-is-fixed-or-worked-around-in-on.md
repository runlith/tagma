title: A Tagma defect blocking work is fixed or worked around in one simple way — ritual, crutches and spending tokens on it are forbidden
ord: 5
tags: ["rule"]
---
Stop work and fix Tagma in its own sources, or temporarily work around the defect with one direct action and record a reflection. Do not rewrite files, repeat calls, or build procedures with extra steps or agents around the defect.

A commit that `submit_work` refuses because of a Tagma defect never stops the work and is never taken to the operator. Keep working, and land the work later or with a plain `git commit`. Never wait on a server restart, a fix or an operator decision because of it.
