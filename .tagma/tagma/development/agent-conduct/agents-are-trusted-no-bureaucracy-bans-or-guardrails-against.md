title: "Agents are trusted: no bureaucracy, bans, or guardrails against our own agents, and no machinery against imaginary threats"
ord: 3
tags: ["rule"]
---
Do not build lock, freeze, pin, proof, confirmation, approval step, ban, or remove suppressions to stop our agents from changing rules, configs, checkers, or code; these are ordinary changes in history. No mechanism is built against an unnamed threat; one costing more than the problem it prevents is removed.
