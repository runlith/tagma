A partial reading answers its requested and affected paths: a changed declaration can make an unchanged caller or literal a new violation. Blob and revision identities let the checks reuse facts across commands and checkouts. The cache reader identity follows the candidate's checker sources and the installed parsers that execute them; changing a declared lockfile alone must not strand prepared indexes. A missing or distant starting index never turns a judgment into a repository scan: the key answers that it is unprepared, and the gate prepares the candidate with one whole reading of its tree and judges again, so the whole cost falls on the first judgment of a reader version and never on a later commit. Preparing HEAD instead could not serve a different candidate reader.

The caller check follows declared capabilities through syntax and local/imported provenance, not a TypeScript type checker. Generic, utility-type and inheritance flows, untyped argument propagation, mutable collection storage and pattern loads are outside its proof. Metadata and lifecycle imports alone are not model calls, and a row's type alone is not a runtime row read.
