title: Waiting on a process or server delivers a verdict on fact, not on deadline expiry
ord: 6
tags: ["rule"]
---
A fact is an answer, a recorded failure, a process exit, an event or a resource release. While the process is alive and holding its own, the wait has no deadline; a request to your own process has no answer deadline. Poll frequency only sets the next question.
