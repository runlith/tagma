What separated the top from the rest was one line of code: `CheckoutReads.containing` resolves `named` through `realPath` and compares it to `row.path` as recorded, while `ProjectStores.at` resolves both sides. The comment above the first says "Canonical on BOTH sides". Opus wrote the asymmetry as a limitation; Muse max, DeepSeek (b), SWE-2, Luna and GLM wrote the comment — five of nine; Sonnet, DeepSeek (a) and Muse xhigh bound their canonical-path claim to `ProjectStores` and said nothing about `containing`. The second separator was the callers' "no unit of work open" obligation: DeepSeek (a) wrote it as the rule it is, Opus left it and said so, and four contestants wrote it as a claim the code cannot establish.

Price, not tokens, is what the ranking is read against. At the list prices the operator supplied on 2026-09-21 (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash via Morph: $0.12 / M input, $0.0036 / M cached, $0.48 / M output; Claude Opus 5 on AWS: $5 / M input, $0.50 / M cached read, $6.25 / M cache write, $25 / M output; Claude Sonnet 5 on AWS: $2 / M input, $0.20 / M cached read, $2.50 / M cache write, $10 / M output), the first-place run cost about $3.83 (3.14M cached reads, 149k cache writes, 53k output), the second-place Sonnet run about $1.39 (2.61M cached reads, 186k cache writes, 40k output), and the fourth-place DeepSeek (a) run about $0.11 — thirty-five times less than Opus and thirteen times less than Sonnet for a draft that was mostly right and one review away from usable; DeepSeek (b) about $0.09. Muse and GLM prices were not supplied; SWE-2 is served without charge at the time of writing. Read against price, the question the ranking answers is not "who is best" but "who is a builder under review" — and there DeepSeek and Muse max stand beside Sonnet.

Time ran from 3m42s (Muse xhigh) to 14m38s (SWE-2); the two Anthropic runs and the two Muse runs finished inside eight minutes except Opus, and every run past eleven minutes (DeepSeek b, GLM, Luna, SWE-2) ranks fifth or lower. Output tokens ran 26k–90k; DeepSeek (a) spent the most of both and ranks fourth.

Two product observations came out of the drafts and are not yet filed anywhere: `coverage-environment` (Muse max) and `destination-reserved` (DeepSeek a) are codes `worktreeRefusal` can produce on the checkout-read road and `CHECKOUT_PLACE_REFUSALS` does not admit, so the road throws instead of refusing.
