Six judges — Astra, Sol, Opus 5, Fable 5.1, Grok 4.6 and Muse 1.3 contributor — rank the anonymized work as fresh agents without orchestrator context. Grok 4.5 solves and does not judge (the operator's word); its family judges through Grok 4.6. The orchestrating Fable instance files no scores: it collects, anonymizes and computes.

- **Blind, with families unmarked.** Shuffled labels, no model names, a judge's own family's work included without marks. The rounds under `ef2ea2ac` observed a model ranking its own answer last; this does not establish that family or position bias is absent.
- **Gates come first.** Typecheck and suite results are decided objectively before judging; judges rank only gate-passing entries.
- **Mandatory reasons.** Every rank carries its argument, and every claimed factual error must be checkable against the tree it was claimed about.
- **Rubric.** Discipline A: owners cut by state, clean direction between files, a coherent residual file, no scaffolding. Discipline C: root-cause exactness, fix at the owner, proof strength.
- **Computed after**: mean rank per model; inter-judge agreement; self-family bias — a judge's rank of its own family against the others' rank of the same entry; and the hidden control's rank per judge.
