title: The delivery review is launched by its author after their own checks, and they sort out the findings themselves
ord: 5
tags: ["rule"]
---
A micro-edit with an obvious result needs no review. A reviewer runs no tests or typechecks; a whole-delivery review covers correctness, architecture, code quality, performance, and check sufficiency. The author confirms a finding along the full code chain before fixing; agreeing model opinions prove no defect, code reading replaces no measurement.
