title: The delivery review is launched by its author after their own checks, and they sort out the findings themselves
ord: 5
tags: ["rule"]
---
A micro-edit with an obvious result needs no review; an instruction from the operator or the launching agent about review outweighs this rule. The reviewers come from the model notes; an unavailable reviewer is skipped, the delivery is not kept waiting. A reviewer is independent and runs no tests or typechecks; a whole-delivery review covers correctness, architecture, code quality, performance, and check sufficiency. The author confirms a finding along the full code chain before fixing; agreeing model opinions prove no defect, code reading replaces no measurement. Review is one round; fixes of confirmed findings go with no second review.
