Every model call in the monorepo runs over the package's own wire, not through a vendor SDK — so every byte and header the provider receives has an owner. The package owns the language a call is written in, the protocols (Anthropic Messages, OpenAI Responses and Chat, Gemini, Bedrock) and the parts an external adapter assembles its own protocol from, transports, the profile contract, model-catalog mechanics, the credential store, and OAuth machinery — with no knowledge of any specific vendor. Each vendor's profiles, model descriptions, sign-in flows, and CLI-login reading live in its own `@runlith/adapter-*` package. The HTTP client, the credential source, and who may spend on what are the product's business.
