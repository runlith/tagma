Question: is semantic search needed when a person remembers the topic but not a single spoken word (Montenegro ↔ Черногория, "поездка" ↔ "Будва")?

The decision must rest on measurement. It needs a set of real operator queries where lexical search with word forms misses, and candidate recall on that set: embeddings, recap synonyms, agent query translation. Until such a set exists, the agent tries other words, and that may be enough.
