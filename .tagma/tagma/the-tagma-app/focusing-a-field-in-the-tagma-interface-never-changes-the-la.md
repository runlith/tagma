title: Focusing a field in the Tagma interface never changes the layout around it; suggestions appear only for typed text, a few at a time
ord: 61
tags: ["rule"]
---
The field keeps its place and size on a click or a shortcut: it does not expand, cover neighbouring controls or open a panel. With nothing typed, nothing is listed; a list of suggestions hangs under the field at its width and holds a handful of rows.
