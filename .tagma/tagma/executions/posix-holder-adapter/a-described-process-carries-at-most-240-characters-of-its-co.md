An argument vector can run to megabytes, and every usage row and evidence snapshot rides a frame of bounded size; an answer past its frame is refused whole. Usage sampling and the Linux evidence probe therefore share one cut, marked with an ellipsis; macOS `ps` already cuts its own column shorter.
