On a wide window a panel under the conversation costs reading height while the side has room, and the side is where the thread's diff already stands. A second dock position — right of the conversation, sharing the side with the diff — would keep both in view. What would decide it: how often the diff and a shell are wanted at once, and whether one seam can host either without two layout owners.
