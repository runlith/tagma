A known calibration — remembered by the worker or stored in the server's inspection cache — is an observation of a small sample, not proof of a new reach's cost: the source count and size could have changed its representativeness. It is reused only while the subject fits it; past that, a fresh bounded sample is taken before any refusal, the decision the kit makes for both the worker and the CLI. The cap itself stays. The sample spreads over the set so a small alphabetical prefix doesn't turn the fixed costs of reading and a cold parser into the price of every byte of a large project; a small change stays the first selection set.
