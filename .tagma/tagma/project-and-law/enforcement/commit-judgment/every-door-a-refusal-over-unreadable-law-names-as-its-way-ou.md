While the vocabulary or a rule's enforcement document does not read, nothing says which rules run before an operation, so every agent operation that changes nothing is judged and refused — except the ways out in `judge.ts`. The refusals over unreadable law send the caller to doors that say why and name each unreadable declaration with how to correct or delete it; every such door belongs to the ways out. A missing recovery door turns diagnosis into a refusal loop. Landings remain available with the unreadable law named as unjudged.
