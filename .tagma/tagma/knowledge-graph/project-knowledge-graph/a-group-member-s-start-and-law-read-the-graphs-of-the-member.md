A thread whose home is a member of a group reads the graphs of the members that reach it, each from that member's own main copy; a member reaches it with its map or its law only when that member gives it and the home takes it (`reaching` over `ProjectGroups`). Prompt law runs global, then the group's rules under the group's name — these reach every member whatever it takes — then the home member's and each reaching member's prompt rules in the group's order under that member's name; the home member's come from the copy the thread stands in. The start draws the global line, the group root naming the home and the members whose map reaches it, the home member's section marked as where the thread stands with its map, then each reaching member's root and top regions.

All of it spends the one map budget of the home project: each reaching member gets an equal share of it for its top regions, and the home map gets what they leave, so a group never grows the start beyond what the project already allows.

A member graph that does not open refuses the start and the law, as the global graph's does: law that cannot be read is withheld context, never silently dropped. A project in no group — and the group's own graph — reads exactly as before.
